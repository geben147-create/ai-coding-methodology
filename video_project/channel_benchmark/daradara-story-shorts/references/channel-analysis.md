## Repeated Script Pattern

1. Open with danger, loss, or an unexplained result.
2. Identify a victim and a socially protected antagonist.
3. Remove normal escape routes: adults do not believe, witnesses stay silent,
   or the protagonist cannot move or speak.
4. Add a second incident with a concrete object or line of dialogue.
5. Let the protagonist prepare privately.
6. Return to the earlier object or line during the reversal.
7. Finish with a consequence and one sentence of moral aftertaste.

The narration uses factual `です/ました` delivery. Common pivots are
`ですが`, `ある日`, `次の瞬間`, `そこで`, and `そして`. Dialogue is rare and
therefore receives extra weight.

## Visual Grammar

- Vertical low-budget 3D human figures
- Long narrow faces and enlarged glossy eyes
- Smooth, wax-like skin and simplified hair
- Thin limbs, stiff elbows, and broad readable gestures
- Basic school, gym, classroom, and outdoor environments
- Mostly hard cuts with little transition decoration
- One spoken phrase per visual setup
- Central yellow Japanese captions with a thick black outline
- Opening white premise panel and red emphasis line
- Close-ups of faces, hands, and impact objects used as punctuation

Observed ten-second samples changed location, character, or action at every
sample. The finer practical target is a 1.2-3.2 second shot, with 3-5 seconds
reserved for a new location.

## Voice And Audio

Every sampled description credits `VOICEVOX:四国めたん`.

Functional voice characteristics:

- bright synthetic female timbre
- neutral but urgent delivery
- steady pitch and limited emotional acting
- dense phrasing with almost no dead air
- narration remains dominant over background music

The public automatic-caption track identifies music around 13.7 seconds and
again near the ending. It does not provide a reliable inventory of individual
sound effects. Exact effect counts therefore remain unverified without access
to a rights-cleared audio source.

Use these confidence labels:

- Voice engine credit: high confidence
- Narration pace and sentence density: high confidence
- Continuous low music bed: medium confidence
- Exact SFX identity and count: low confidence

## Hook And Retention Details

- The title promises a complete story, not a topic explanation.
- The opening reveals consequence before context.
- Proper names and exact quantities make the story feel concrete.
- A new unanswered question appears before the previous one fully resolves.
- The most quoted line returns during the payoff.
- The ending resolves status and emotion without a separate subscribe CTA.

## Originality Boundary

Do not reuse the benchmark's school-bully plot, names, injuries, dialogue,
training sequence, three-brother setup, or final medical consequence. Reuse
only abstract functions such as failed authority, quiet preparation, echoed
dialogue, and final status reversal.
