## Reusable Structure

1. Show the strongest half-second from the best entry.
2. State the category and number of entries immediately.
3. Count down or clearly label every entry.
4. Give each clip enough setup, action, and reaction to remain understandable.
5. Increase novelty or emotion toward the final entry.
6. End on the complete reaction, not a premature cut.

Suggested clip allocation:

| Rank count | Total length | Per entry |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 25-36 s | 7-10 s |
| 4 | 30-45 s | 6-9 s |
| 5 | 35-50 s | 5-8 s |
| 6 | 40-55 s | 5-7 s |

Use a 0.5-1.2 second opening hook and 0.2-0.5 second rank cards. Prefer hard
cuts, short whooshes, and one musical beat per rank change.

## Rights-Safe Replacement

Do not download or republish the benchmark channel's clips. Build ranking
videos from:

- user-owned recordings
- creator-supplied files with written permission
- licensed stock
- explicit Creative Commons sources
- public-domain footage
- original animation or reenactment

Record the source and output time ranges for every entry.
